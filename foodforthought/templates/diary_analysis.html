How to read this. The RNI (Reference Nutrient Intake) is enough for about 97.5% of people. The LRNI (Lower Reference Nutrient Intake) is enough for only the 2.5% with the lowest needs, so intake below it is very likely to be too low. Intake between the LRNI and the RNI may be enough for some people and not for others. These describe what was eaten on the diary days, not whether the client is deficient: a diary cannot diagnose a deficiency.
